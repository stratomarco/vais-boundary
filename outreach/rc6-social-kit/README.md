# VAIS RC6 social kit

This is an informal outreach kit for finding critical reviewers, not a product announcement or endorsement campaign.

## Contents

- `POST-1-LINKEDIN.md`: the main LinkedIn/general post.
- `POST-2-SHORT.md`: a shorter version for other social platforms.
- `FOLLOW-UP-MESSAGE.md`: a direct reply for people who ask to review the work.

## Before posting

Read the message once in your own voice and replace any phrase you would not normally use. Do not describe zero observed protected violations as proof of security. Keep the model-configuration exception and bounded-evidence caveat intact.

The benchmark image identifies the evidence as VAIS 0.12.0rc5 rendered by 0.12.0rc6. It keeps protected violations, workflow utility and attack-added diagnostic activity separate; it is not a model-safety leaderboard or composite score.

When someone responds, send the code-free reviewer pack privately rather than posting source code or raw evidence publicly.
