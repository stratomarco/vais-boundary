# Medium article brief

## Working title

We Let Fifteen Local Models Plan Under Attack. The Security Boundary Was Deterministic.

## Central argument

Prompt injection is difficult to eliminate at the model layer. A system can instead assume model compromise, restrict the model to proposals, authorize effects through deterministic controls, and measure security at independently observed protected outcomes.

## Narrative

1. The problem: model behavior changes are routinely confused with system compromise.
2. The VAIS thesis: `behavioral change != attack success != protected impact`.
3. The experiment: fifteen diverse Q4_K_M local models, twenty matched control/attack stories and staged adaptive campaigns on one 16 GB GPU.
4. The measurement: protected violations, workflow utility, attack-added diagnostic events, failures and configuration conformance remain separate.
5. The result: fourteen full completions, 3,360 balanced full episodes, substantial utility/drift variation, and zero observed protected violations across 4,299 evaluable staged executions.
6. The exception: DeepSeek stopped at the reasoning-off gate and receives no inferred full result.
7. What the result means: deterministic enforcement contained observed model-side failures in this bounded test.
8. What it does not mean: no universal-security proof, no causal model ranking and no claim that denial activity is model safety.
9. What comes next: stability repeats, a separately labeled reasoning-enabled cohort and independent reproduction.
