# Paper / Article Notes

Potential central framing:

> AI security is not security AI. VAIS treats the model as an untrusted component and applies conventional security engineering around its authority, data flows and observable effects.

1. Threat model: assume the model can be influenced or compromised.
2. Deterministic security boundary: task authority, scopes, exact approvals, provenance, confidentiality and complete mediation.
3. Measurement discipline: separate plan change, escalation, objective success and protected impact.
4. Stateful reference system: multi-step effects, tenant boundaries, declassification and delegation.
5. Adaptive verification: reward only independently observed protected invariant violation.
6. Empirical results: direct context, MCP delivery, reference-system traces and adaptive campaigns.
7. Measurement corrections and negative results.
8. Limitations and remaining TCB attack surface.
